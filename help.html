REVISIÉ GUIDE

Help centre.

A quick reference for making the most of your revision workspace.

Flashcards

Choose a subject and unit, reveal each answer, then mark whether you knew it. Use “Make a flashcard” to add your own question to any unit.

Notes

Notes are tagged to a subject and appear on both its subject page and the Notes page. You can edit or delete saved notes.

Photo to text

Upload a clear photo of handwritten or printed notes. REVISIÉ improves contrast, then OCR turns the image into editable text. OCR needs an internet connection.

Streaks

Your streak is a small daily commitment, not a punishment. A short five-minute review is enough to keep momentum alive.

Study assistant

Ask for an explanation, a quiz, or a note summary. The current offline assistant uses your subject deck and saved notes in this browser.

Your data

Custom subjects, flashcards, notes, and planner checks are stored locally in your browser. They do not sync between devices yet.

GCSE HIGHER TIER

Exam-board profiles

REVISIÉ is currently organized around the GCSE Higher Tier subjects offered through the SHSB curriculum. Always check your current school entry confirmation for the exact paper and specification.
