Study and practise

Subjects and topics

Choose a subject to find its flashcards, quizzes, notes and PDFs. Add your own subjects and topics, rename them, and change their colours, symbols or exam boards. New and edited flashcards automatically feed the topic’s quizzes.

Open subjects

Flashcards and imports

Open a topic’s deck, reveal each answer, then choose I knew this or Review again. Add or edit your own cards, or import a set by pasting text or uploading TXT, TSV, CSV or JSON. Preview the cards before saving. The import window includes a prompt and Quizlet export instructions.

Open flashcards

Multiple-choice quizzes

Flashcards provide two matching forms: choose the saved answer, or identify the saved question from its answer. Options come from other cards in the same subject; automatic matching needs at least two distinct saved answers. Submit to see the correct option in green and a wrong selection in red, with text labels and an explanation.

Start multiple choice

Short-answer quizzes

Write your answer from memory, then reveal the model answer and marking points. Choose I got it right or Needs practice after comparing the key points. This is self-marking, and results are labelled that way. Your current flashcards appear automatically, including cards in topics you add manually.

Start short answer

Varied maths practice

Your maths topics appear alongside practice skills grouped into Number, Algebra, Geometry and measures, and Statistics and probability. Skills are suggested from the topic name and flashcards; use Choose skills to change them or enable an unmatched topic. Each session mixes 10 questions with fresh numbers, direct problems, missing values and applications. Answers accept numbers or fractions, with rounding instructions where needed, and include worked solutions.

Practise maths

Make a question set

Use Generate / import in either quiz library. Choose the topic, question types (including Mixed), number, difficulty and course. Your topic’s flashcards are included by default; you can use notes instead. Copy the prompt into your AI chat, paste its response back, then preview, deselect unwanted questions and save. REVISIÉ prepares the prompt and imports the response; rewritten sets are created in your AI chat.

Generate / import questions

Write or edit quiz questions

Choose Write a question to create a multiple-choice or short-answer question yourself. Multiple choice needs four different options, one correct option and an explanation. Short answer needs a model answer or marking points. Manage / add lets you edit or delete questions you wrote or imported. Adding more flashcards also expands the automatic question bank.

Make your own questions

Organise your revision

Folders

Group decks, notes and PDFs for an exam or revision goal. Use Add to folder on material, or Add material inside a folder. One item can belong to several folders. Revise all flashcards combines the folder’s decks. Removing a link or deleting a folder keeps the original material. Guest folders stay in this browser; signed-in folders sync.

Open folders

Notes and photos

Save notes under a subject to find them on its page and in Notes. Attach photos, edit your writing or remove notes you no longer need. If a saved photo does not load, open the note and use Retry photo. Saved written notes can also supply the material for a quiz-generation prompt.

Open notes

Photo to text

Add a clear photo of handwritten or printed notes in the notes editor, then choose Extract text from photo to turn it into editable writing. This needs an internet connection. Check the recognised text before saving it.

Open notes editor

PDF resources

Upload PDFs and assign them to a subject. Open or download a saved resource when you revise, or organise it in a folder. Sign in to save uploaded files with your account and access them on another device.

Open resources

Revision planner

Add a session with a subject, date, start time and duration. Tap a session to edit it, or drag it to another slot on desktop. Tap its circle to mark it complete: a green tick and Completed label show its state. Tap again to undo. The control also works with Enter or Space. Completing a session dated today counts towards your streak.

Open planner

Your workspace and account

What the menu numbers mean

Subjects shows the subject total. Flashcards shows cards remaining to review. Multiple Choice and Short Answer show available questions. Maths Practice counts your maths subject topics. Folders, Notes and Resources show saved item totals; Revision planner shows incomplete sessions. Counts refresh as you add, edit, remove or complete work, and library pages show the corresponding workspace totals.

Browse your workspace

Progress and study streaks

Review a flashcard, answer a quiz question or complete today’s planner session to earn a study day. Days follow your local calendar; missing a day resets the current streak, while your personal best stays saved. Progress combines recorded flashcard review time with completed planner sessions. Quiz scores stay in each quiz library’s Recent results and do not change flashcard mastery or its study-time counter.

View progress

Share your revision

Sign in and use Share on a note, PDF, deck or subject to create a link. Someone opening it can preview the material and sign in to save a copy. On Overview, Progress or the streak popup, Share streak lets you preview and share or download an image card.

Open your subjects

Saving and syncing

Guests’ work stays in the current browser. Sign in to sync your saved workspace, quiz questions and results, maths skill choices, progress, settings and streak across devices. Local changes can sync when you reconnect while signed in. Overview can reopen your last activity, including an unfinished flashcard review.

Log in or sign up

Appearance and automatic logos

Settings offers Light, Dark or System appearance; System follows your device. The logo changes automatically for Pride in June and UK Black History Month in October, using matching light- or dark-mode artwork. Other months use the normal logo. You can also change your name or photo and reduce interface motion. Choose Save settings to apply your preferences.

Open settings

Password and account

Use Forgot password? on the login screen to request a reset email, or request one in Settings. Follow the email link to set a new password. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account, enter your current password and type DELETE. Account deletion permanently removes your saved workspace, uploaded files and share links.
