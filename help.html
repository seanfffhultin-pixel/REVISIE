REVISIÉ GUIDE

Help centre.

A quick reference for making the most of your revision workspace.

Subjects and exam boards

Browse Subjects, add your own subjects and topics, or use a subject’s options to rename topics and change its colour or symbol. Exam boards start as “Exam board”; choose your board from the subject options.

Open subjects

Review and create flashcards

Choose a subject and topic, reveal an answer, then mark it Known or Again. Make your own flashcards or edit saved cards. Review time saves automatically while the flashcards are open and the tab is visible, including sessions you close early. Progress shows this time alongside completed planner sessions. On Overview, Pick up where you left off reopens your last activity and continues an unfinished review from its remaining cards.

Open flashcards

Import flashcards

Use Import flashcards to paste a set or upload TXT, TSV, CSV or JSON. Choose the destination subject and topic, check the preview, and import. The import window includes instructions for Quizlet exports and an AI prompt.

Import a set

Notes and photos

Save notes under a subject to find them on its subject page and in Notes. Attach a photo, edit your writing, or remove notes you no longer need. If a saved photo fails to load, open the note and use Retry photo.

Open notes

Photo to text

Upload a clear photo of handwritten or printed notes in the notes editor, then use photo-to-text to turn it into editable writing. This needs an internet connection. Check the recognised text before saving.

Open notes editor

PDF resources

Upload PDFs to keep revision resources with your subjects. Open or download a saved resource when you need it. Sign in to save uploaded files with your account and access them on another device.

Open resources

Revision planner

Add a study session with a subject, date, start time and duration. Edit it as your plans change, or drag it to another time slot. Mark a session complete when you finish; completing a session dated today counts towards your streak.

Open planner

Build and share your streak

Review a flashcard or complete today’s planner session to earn one study day. Days follow your local calendar; missing a full day resets the current streak, while your personal best stays saved. On Overview, Progress or the streak popup, choose Share streak to preview a card, then share or download it for a message or story.

View your streak

Share revision materials

Use Share on a note, PDF, deck or subject to create a share link. Sign in to create links. Someone opening a link can preview the item and sign in to save a copy to their own workspace. A streak shares as an image card.

Browse your subjects

Saving and syncing

Sign in to sync your saved revision workspace, study progress, settings and streak across devices. Signed-out work stays in the current browser. If you lose your connection, local changes can sync when you reconnect while signed in.

Log in or sign up

Profile and preferences

Settings lets you change your display name, add or remove a profile photo, choose Light, Dark or System appearance, and reduce interface motion. System follows your device’s theme. Choose Save settings to apply your changes.

Open settings

Password and account

Forgot your password? Choose Forgot password? on the login screen and follow the email link to set a new one. You can also request a reset email in Settings. To delete your account, go to Settings → Delete account, enter your current password and type DELETE. Deletion permanently removes your account, saved workspace, uploaded files and share links.

Manage your account

Check your specification

The built-in subjects and starter flashcards give you a place to begin. Set your own exam board and check topics and answers against your school’s current specification. You can add, rename and remove topics to suit your course.
